How it works

From concept to scan

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We talk about your menu, your vibe, and how guests find you — table QR, website, social, or all three.

  2. 02

    Creative direction

    I storyboard the flow: how dishes appear, how sections scroll, what makes someone stop and watch.

  3. 03

    Production

    Shoot or source footage, edit for mobile, and build the interactive menu experience.

  4. 04

    Launch

    You get QR assets, a hosted link, and embed code for your website. Ready for service.

Why video menus

Static PDFs don't sell the sizzle

Guests decide with their eyes first. A video menu shows texture, portion size, and craft — the things a flat list never captures. It's the difference between reading about food and craving it.

  • Higher engagement than traditional menus
  • Easy updates when items or prices change
  • Works for fine dining, casual, bars, and pop-ups
  • One link — table, takeout bag, Instagram bio
